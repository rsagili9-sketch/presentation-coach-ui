Metric prints measured numbers: words per minute, filler rate, duration, longest pause and the filler list. The consumer provides computed values (never model-written ones), their units and, for fillers, their timestamps.

A number is large serif (`stat`) with its unit in `caption` below. A missing value shows **Unavailable**, never 0 or a dash. Measured numbers sit under the MEASURED label; any model-written sentence sits under AI COMMENT in the same card, after a hairline rule. Never put the two in one sentence.
