CriterionCard shows one rubric criterion on Results. The consumer provides the criterion name, earned points, maximum points, an explanation and one transcript quote with its timestamp.

- Earned and maximum points are shown as `7 / 10`, with a thin bar for position. The bar is ink, never coral.
- The explanation is an AI COMMENT; the quote is evidence and always carries a `coral-text` timestamp.
- A criterion the audio can't support reads **Not assessable**, shows no score or bar, and is excluded from the total and percentage.

Don't invent a quote. If none fits, say "No clear moment in the transcript" in the explanation.
