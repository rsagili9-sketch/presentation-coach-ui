TakeRow is one saved take in History. The consumer provides the take name, date, rubric name (or "No rubric · Provisional"), duration, and the app-calculated score as `earned / maximum`.

Rows sit in one `surface-raised` card separated by hairlines, newest first. Ticking two rows enables "Compare these two"; a third tick replaces the oldest. A take without a score shows **Unavailable**. No badges, no avatars.
